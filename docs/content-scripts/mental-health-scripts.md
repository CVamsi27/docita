# Mental Health Scripts

Short-form video scripts covering stress, anxiety, burnout, work-life balance, digital detox, therapy stigma, and mental wellness. Each script is 15–60 seconds.

---

### Short 1: Stress Is Not a Badge

Hook text:

```text
Being "always busy" is not a personality. It is a health risk.
```

Shots:

1. Person juggling phone, laptop, notifications.
2. Person sitting quietly, doing nothing.
3. Person resting without guilt.

Voiceover:

```text
We wear stress like a badge. "I am so busy" is how we prove we matter.

But chronic stress raises cortisol, weakens immunity, disrupts sleep, and damages relationships.

Rest is not lazy. Rest is maintenance. Schedule it like you schedule meetings.

General information only. Consult a healthcare professional.
```

On-screen text:

- "Stress is not a badge"
- "Rest is maintenance"
- "General info. Not medical advice."

Platform notes: LinkedIn and Instagram. Anti-hustle culture angle.

### Short 8: Breathing When Everything Feels Heavy

Hook text:

```text
When your chest feels tight, your breath is the first thing to fix.
```

Shots:

1. Person clutching chest in stress.
2. 4-7-8 breathing pattern visual.
3. Person calm after breathing exercise.

Voiceover:

```text
When anxiety hits, your breathing becomes shallow and fast. That signals your brain to stay in panic mode.

Try the 4-7-8 technique: inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7, exhale for 8. Repeat four times.

This activates your parasympathetic nervous system. Your body shifts from panic to calm in under a minute.

General information only. Consult a healthcare professional.
```

On-screen text:

- "4-7-8 Breathing"
- "In 4. Hold 7. Out 8."
- "4 rounds. Feel the shift."

Platform notes: Works on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Calming visuals.

### Short 9: Journaling Is Free Therapy

Hook text:

```text
Write it down. It takes the weight out of your head.
```

Shots:

1. Person overwhelmed with thoughts.
2. Person writing in a notebook.
3. Open notebook with simple entries.

Voiceover:

```text
Journaling is one of the simplest mental health tools. It does not need to be poetic or structured.

Write three things: what happened today, how you feel, and what you need.

Five minutes before bed. No phone. Just pen and paper. It externalizes your thoughts and reduces rumination.

General information only. Consult a healthcare professional.
```
