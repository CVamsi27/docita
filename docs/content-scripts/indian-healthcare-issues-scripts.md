# Indian Healthcare Issues Scripts

Short-form video scripts covering systemic issues in Indian healthcare: generic medicine, handwritten prescriptions, access gaps, insurance, rural health, and technology. Each script is 15–60 seconds.

---

### Short 1: Generic Medicine Saves Money

Hook text:

```text
Your doctor prescribed the right medicine. The brand name is costing you 5x more.
```

Shots:

1. Expensive branded medicine strip.
2. Generic version of the same medicine.
3. Price comparison.

Voiceover:

```text
Generic medicines have the same salt, same dose, same effect as branded versions. The difference is the price.

A branded paracetamol strip costs 30 rupees. The generic version costs 5. Same composition.

Ask your pharmacist for the generic alternative. Or ask your doctor to prescribe by salt name, not brand name.

General information only. Consult your doctor or pharmacist.
```

On-screen text:

- "Same salt. Same dose. 5x cheaper."
- "Ask for the generic version"
- "General info. Not medical advice."

Platform notes: Strong Indian audience resonance. Instagram Reels and Shorts.
