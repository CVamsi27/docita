# Patient Education Scripts

Short-form video scripts teaching patients about when to see a doctor, preventive care, vaccination, follow-ups, health checkups, and warning signs. Each script is 15–60 seconds.

---

### Short 1: When to See a Doctor for Fever

Hook text:

```text
Not every fever needs a doctor. But some fevers do.
```

Shots:

1. Person with mild fever.
2. Thermometer showing temperature.
3. Doctor's clinic.

Voiceover:

```text
A mild fever under 100 degrees Fahrenheit with no other symptoms can usually be managed at home: rest, fluids, and paracetamol.

See a doctor if: the fever exceeds 103, lasts more than three days, comes with a rash, severe headache, stiff neck, or difficulty breathing.

When in doubt, get it checked. A 10-minute visit can prevent a bigger problem.

General information only. Consult a healthcare professional.
```

On-screen text:

- "Below 100°F: rest + fluids"
- "Above 103°F or 3+ days: see a doctor"
- "General info. Not medical advice."

Platform notes: Works on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Practical, non-alarming tone.

### Short 2: Chest Pain Is Always Serious

Hook text:

```text
If you feel chest pain, do not Google it. Call for help.
```

Shots:

1. Person clutching chest.
2. Phone dialing emergency number.
3. Ambulance arriving.

Voiceover:

```text
Chest pain can be a sign of a heart attack, especially if it is: pressure or tightness in the center of the chest, pain radiating to the left arm or jaw, shortness of breath, sweating, or nausea.

Call 108 or your local emergency number immediately. Do not drive yourself. Chew an aspirin if you are not allergic.

Every minute counts.

General information only. This is not a substitute for emergency care.
```

On-screen text:

- "Chest pain + arm pain + sweating = emergency"
- "Call 108. Do not drive."
- "Every minute counts."

Platform notes: Urgency hook. Works on all platforms. Life-saving information.

### Short 15: Skin Changes Need Attention

Hook text:

```text
A mole that changes shape is not "just a mark". Get it checked.
```

Shots:

1. Mole or skin spot.
2. ABCDE rule visual.
3. Dermatologist exam.

Voiceover:

```text
Skin cancer is one of the most treatable cancers when caught early. Use the ABCDE rule: Asymmetry, Border irregularity, Color variation, Diameter larger than 6mm, and Evolving size or shape.

If any mole or skin spot matches these, see a dermatologist.

Also see a doctor for: non-healing sores, new growths, or sudden changes in existing spots.

General information only. Consult a healthcare professional.
```

### Short 17: Children's Fever Red Flags

Hook text:

```text
A child's fever needs faster attention than an adult's. Know the red flags.
```

Shots:

1. Child with fever.
2. Parent checking temperature.
3. Doctor examining child.

Voiceover:

```text
For children: see a doctor immediately if the fever is above 102, if the child is under 3 months old with any fever, if there is a rash, seizures, difficulty breathing, or the child is unusually sleepy or irritable.

Do not give aspirin to children under 16. Use paracetamol as directed by your doctor.

When a child is sick, trust your instinct. If something feels wrong, get it checked.

General information only. Consult a healthcare professional.
```

- "Child fever: 102+ or under 3 months = see doctor"
- "No aspirin under 16"

### Short 18: Medication Timing Matters

Hook text:

```text
Taking medicine at the wrong time can make it useless.
```

Shots:

1. Medicine strip with timing instructions.
2. Person taking medicine with food.
3. Person taking medicine on empty stomach.

Voiceover:

```text

Some medicines work better with food. Others need an empty stomach. Some must be taken in the morning. Others at bedtime.

Metformin: with food. Thyroid medicine: empty stomach, morning. Blood pressure medicine: usually morning. Antibiotics: evenly spaced through the day.

Read the label. Ask the pharmacist. Timing matters as much as dosage.

General information only. Consult a healthcare professional.
```

On-screen text:

- "Metformin: with food"
- "Thyroid: empty stomach, morning"
- "Read the label."
- "General info. Not medical advice."

Platform notes: Works on Instagram Reels and LinkedIn. Practical information.
