# Healthy Living Scripts

General wellness tips for short-form video content. Each script is 15–60 seconds, designed for Reels, Shorts, LinkedIn, and X.

### Short 1: The 7-Minute Morning

Hook text:

```text
Your morning sets the rest of your day. Here is a 7-minute version.
```

Shots:

1. Person waking up, stretching.
2. Glass of water.
3. Brief walk or stretch.
4. Simple breakfast.
5. Leaving the house calm.

Voiceover:

```text
Most people start the day scrolling their phone. That trains the brain to react, not to lead.

Try this: wake up, drink a glass of water, do five minutes of light stretching or walking, eat something simple, and check your phone last.

Seven minutes. No app needed. No gym needed. Just a better start.

Built for clinics that care about getting the basics right. Docita — docita.work.

General information only. Not medical advice.
```

On-screen text:

- "7-Minute Morning Routine"
- "Water. Move. Eat. Then phone."
- "Docita — docita.work"

Platform notes: Works best on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Bright morning visuals.

### Short 10: Breathing Is Free Medicine

Hook text:

```text
Stress is not in your head. It is in your breathing.
```

Shots:

1. Person breathing shallowly at desk.
2. Box breathing visual (inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4).
3. Person visibly calmer.

Voiceover:

```text
When you are stressed, your breathing becomes shallow and fast. That tells your brain to stay in fight-or-flight mode.

Fix: inhale for four seconds, hold for four, exhale for four, hold for four. Repeat five times.

Box breathing is used by athletes, pilots, and doctors. It works because it is simple.

General information only. Not medical advice.
```

On-screen text:

- "Box Breathing"
- "In 4 — Hold 4 — Out 4 — Hold 4"
- "5 rounds. Feel the shift."

Platform notes: Works on all platforms. Calming visuals preferred.
