# Doctor Wellness Scripts

Short-form video scripts covering doctor burnout, admin burden, time management, career growth, and work-life balance for clinicians. Each script is 15–60 seconds.

---

### Short 1: Doctor Burnout Is an Epidemic

Hook text:

```text
Doctors save lives. But who saves the doctor?
```

Shots:

1. Doctor looking exhausted at end of day.
2. Late-night paperwork.
3. Doctor with family, relaxed.

Voiceover:

```text
Studies show that up to 50 percent of doctors experience burnout at some point in their career. Long hours, emotional weight, administrative burden, and patient expectations pile up.

Burnout does not make you a bad doctor. It makes you a human one.

Recognize it early: exhaustion, cynicism, detachment, feeling ineffective. Then act: rest, boundaries, support, and sometimes a career change.

General information only. Consult a mental health professional.
```

On-screen text:

- "50% of doctors experience burnout"
- "Recognize. Act. Recover."
- "General info. Not medical advice."

Platform notes: Strong on LinkedIn. Doctor audience.

### Short 12: Sleep Debt Is Real for Doctors

Hook text:

```text
You tell patients to sleep 7 hours. You sleep 5. That catches up.
```

Shots:

1. Doctor on night duty.
2. Doctor sleeping during the day.
3. Doctor catching up on sleep.

Voiceover:

```text
Doctors, especially those on call or night shifts, often sleep far less than they recommend to patients.

Sleep debt accumulates: impaired judgment, slower reflexes, increased errors, and long-term health consequences.

Prioritize sleep like you would a critical patient. It is not selfish. It is patient safety.

General information only. Consult a healthcare professional.
```

On-screen text:
