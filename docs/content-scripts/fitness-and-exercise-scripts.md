# Fitness and Exercise Scripts

Short-form video scripts for home workouts, office ergonomics, walking, yoga, stretching, and general fitness. Each script is 15–60 seconds.

---

### Short 1: Home Workout, No Equipment

Hook text:

```text
Your living room is a gym. You just forgot.
```

Shots:

1. Empty living room.
2. Person doing squats, push-ups, lunges.
3. Person sweaty but done.

Voiceover:

```text
You do not need a gym membership or fancy equipment. Your body weight is enough.

Ten squats. Ten push-ups. Ten lunges. Thirty second plank. Repeat three rounds.

That is a full body workout in under fifteen minutes. No equipment. No commute. No excuse.

General information only. Consult a healthcare professional before starting exercise.
```

On-screen text:

- "10 squats. 10 push-ups. 10 lunges."
- "30-sec plank. 3 rounds."
- "Docita — docita.work"

Platform notes: Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Energetic visuals.

### Short 2: Office Desk Stretches

Hook text:

```text
Your body was not designed to sit for 8 hours. Stretch before it locks up.
```

Shots:

1. Person hunched at desk.
2. Neck rolls.
3. Shoulder shrugs.
4. Seated spinal twist.

Voiceover:

```text
Every two hours at your desk, do this: roll your neck five times each direction. Shrug your shoulders up and hold for five seconds. Twist your torso left and right. Stretch your wrists forward and back.

Four moves. Two minutes. Your body stays functional through the workday.

General information only. Not medical advice.
```

On-screen text:

- "Every 2 hours: 4 moves, 2 minutes"
- "Neck. Shoulders. Spine. Wrists."
- "General info. Not medical advice."

### Short 11: Breathing Exercise for Stress

Hook text:

```text
Your breath is the fastest way to calm your nervous system.
```

Shots:

1. Person stressed at desk.
2. Deep belly breathing visual.
3. Person calm after breathing exercise.

Voiceover:

```text
When you are stressed, your breathing becomes shallow. That keeps your body in fight-or-flight mode.

Fix: place one hand on your chest, one on your belly. Breathe in through your nose so only the belly hand moves. Breathe out slowly through your mouth.

Five rounds of this activates your parasympathetic nervous system. Your body calms down.

General information only. Not medical advice.
```

On-screen text:

- "Belly breathing"
- "Hand on chest, hand on belly"
- "5 rounds to calm down"
- "Docita — docita.work"


1. Person doing sit-ups with poor form.
