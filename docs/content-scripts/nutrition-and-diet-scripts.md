# Nutrition and Diet Scripts

Short-form video scripts covering balanced meals, protein, fiber, Indian diet myths, supplements, deficiencies, and meal prep. Each script is 15–60 seconds.

### Short 1: Protein Is Not Just for Gym People

Hook text:

```text
Protein is not a gym supplement. It is a basic nutrient your body needs every day.
```

Shots:

1. Eggs, dal, paneer, chicken, fish on a plate.
2. Person eating a protein-rich meal.
3. Label showing protein content.

Voiceover:

```text
Most Indians do not eat enough protein. The recommended intake is about 0.8 grams per kilogram of body weight.

For a 60 kilogram person, that is roughly 48 grams per day. Two eggs, a bowl of dal, and some paneer or chicken gets you close.

You do not need protein powder. You need balanced meals.

General information only. Consult a healthcare professional.
```

On-screen text:

- "0.8g per kg body weight"
- "Dal. Eggs. Paneer. Chicken."
- "General info. Not medical advice."

Platform notes: Works on Instagram Reels and LinkedIn. Food visuals.

### Short 2: Fiber Is the Missing Nutrient

Hook text:

```text
Your gut is begging for fiber. Most Indian diets do not have enough.
```

Shots:

1. Plate with white rice and curry only.
2. Plate with vegetables, salad, and whole grains.
3. Fruits, vegetables, and legumes.

Voiceover:

```text
The average Indian adult eats about 15 grams of fibre per day. The recommended intake is 25 to 30 grams.

Fibre helps digestion, controls blood sugar, lowers cholesterol, and keeps you full longer.

Add one extra serving of vegetables or fruit per day. Switch from white rice to brown rice or millets occasionally. Add sprouts or legumes to lunch.

General information only. Not medical advice.
```

### Short 7: Oil Is Not Poison

Hook text:

```text
You do not need to stop using oil. You need to use less of it.
```

Shots:

1. Person pouring large amount of oil.
2. Measuring spoon with oil.
3. Different oils: mustard, coconut, olive.

Voiceover:

```text
All cooking oils are fats. Some are healthier than others, but none should be poured freely.

The recommended intake is about 3 to 4 teaspoons per day for an adult. Most Indian households use two to three times that.

Measure your oil. Use mustard or sesame oil for Indian cooking. Do not deep fry when you can saute or steam.

General information only. Not medical advice.
```

### Short 20: Eat Slowly

Hook text:

```text
It takes 20 minutes for your brain to know you are full. Speed eating defeats that.
```

Shots:

1. Person eating quickly.
2. Clock showing 20 minutes.
3. Person eating slowly, chewing properly.

Voiceover:

```text
Your stomach sends fullness signals to your brain after about 20 minutes. If you finish a meal in five, you have eaten more than you needed before your brain even knew.

Chew each bite. Put the spoon down between bites. Talk to someone at the table.

Eating slowly improves digestion and reduces overeating.

General information only. Not medical advice.
```

On-screen text:

- "20 minutes for full signal"
- "Chew. Pause. Breathe."
- "Docita — docita.work"

Platform notes: Works on all platforms. Simple, actionable tip.
