## Positioning

Do not open with "please use our software." Open with the operational pain:

- Clinics lose time because appointments, patient notes, billing, prescriptions, and WhatsApp messages live in separate places.
- Doctors do not want more admin work. They want patient context, prescription flow, and fewer interruptions.
- Facility owners want visibility: what happened today, who is waiting, what was billed, and what follow-up is pending.
- Docita should feel like a healthcare operating layer, not just appointment software.

Core message:

```text
Docita helps clinics and hospitals run appointments, patient records, consultations, prescriptions, invoices, follow-ups, WhatsApp workflows, and facility operations from one connected workspace.
```

Adoption message:

```text
Start small. Set up one doctor, one front desk user, and today's appointments. You do not need to digitize the entire facility on day one.
```

### The one idea every product video must land

Whatever the topic, the viewer should walk away understanding **connection**: when the front desk books, the doctor sees it; when the doctor consults, billing sees it; the prescription is tied to the patient; the invoice is tied to the visit; the follow-up stays visible. Nothing falls through the cracks because nothing is disconnected. If a cut does not reinforce connection, it is decoration — keep it short or drop it.

### Audience map

| Audience | What they fear | What to show | Primary screens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo doctor | "More admin, more typing" | Patient context appears instantly; prescription in seconds | Today, Patient detail, Consultation, Prescriptions |

| Evaluator / buyer | "Will my real day fit?" | One patient journey end to end | Public booking → Consultation → Invoice → Follow-up |

## Compliance guardrails

- Use demo data only.
- Do not show real patient names, phone numbers, addresses, prescriptions, lab reports, invoices, ABHA details, or WhatsApp conversations.
- Blur browser bookmarks, email, phone notifications, local file paths, account IDs, API keys, and admin-only diagnostics.
- Avoid claims like "guaranteed revenue increase", "fully compliant for every hospital", or "replaces medical judgment."
- Use the phrase "where enabled" for WhatsApp, payments, patient portal, ABHA, insurance, lab, and hospital workflows if the video is not tied to a specific active plan.
- Include a small footer on clinical demo videos:

```text
Demo data shown. Docita supports clinical workflow documentation and does not replace medical judgment.
```

- Do not claim certifications (NABL, ABDM, ISO, HIPAA) unless a specific, current certificate is in hand. Say "built with healthcare access control, audit logging, and privacy expectations in mind" instead.
- Pricing and trial terms ("30-day free trial", "no credit card") must match the live offer on `docita.work` at publish time. If the offer changes, re-record or caption over it — never imply a permanent offer.

## Standard video templates

### Long-form template

Use for YouTube, website embeds, sales follow-ups, and WhatsApp demo links.

Length:

- Short long form: 4 to 6 minutes.
- Full product tour: 8 to 12 minutes.
- Webinar style: 18 to 25 minutes.

Structure:

1. **Hook, 0:00–0:15** — State the clinic problem in one sentence. Show a fast visual preview of the final workflow.
2. **Context, 0:15–0:40** — Who this is for (solo doctor, clinic, hospital, front desk, owner, admin) and what will be shown.
3. **Demo, 0:40 to ~80% of runtime** — Move through real screens in workflow order. Zoom on fields, buttons, queue rows, prescription preview, invoice preview, WhatsApp templates.
4. **Recap, final 30–45s** — Repeat the operational benefit, not the feature list.
5. **CTA, final 10–15s** — Show the contact card.

Visual format:

- Record screen at 16:9, 1920x1080 minimum.
- Browser zoom 90–100%.
- Cursor visible, movement slow and intentional.
- Clean demo account, no browser extensions visible.
- Editor adds 120–140% zoom punches for important clicks.
- Captions on every video.

Audio:

- Voiceover first, music second.
- Music low, -24 to -30 LUFS under voice.
- Calm, modern, light, trust-building music.
- Avoid aggressive EDM, comic SFX, dramatic risers, distracting beats.

Outro card:

```text
Run your clinic workflow from one place.

Docita
Visit us at https://docita.work  ·  mail us at support@docita.work  ·  contact us at +91 87903 11010
```

### Short-form template

Use for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, LinkedIn short video, and X video.

Length:

- Best range: 15 to 45 seconds.
- Demo explainer: 45 to 75 seconds.
- Ad-style versions under 60 to 90 seconds.

Structure:

1. **Hook, 0:00–0:03** — On-screen text must be readable without sound.
2. **Proof visual, 0:03–0:10** — Show the screen that solves the problem.
3. **Micro-demo, 0:10–0:30** — One workflow only. Do not tour the whole app.
4. **CTA, final 3–5s** — Show contact card or pinned caption.

Visual format:

- Edit in 9:16, 1080x1920.
- Keep all text inside the central safe zone.
- Big captions, 2 lines maximum.
- Fast but clean cuts: one idea per shot.
- Use zoom crops from the long-form recording where possible (record once, cut many).

Caption style:

- Font: clean sans-serif, medium or semibold.
- Color: white or near-white on a dark translucent strip.
- Highlight one keyword per sentence in the Docita brand accent color if available.
- Do not cover buttons, patient names, prescription content, or invoice totals.

Outro card for shorts:

```text
Docita
Clinic workflows in one place
docita.work
```

## How to read the short-form scene tables

Every short-form script in [`product-short-form-scripts.md`](product-short-form-scripts.md) uses the same elaborated scene format so the person recording can execute with zero guesswork. Each scene row tells you exactly what to capture and how to get there:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Scene** | The shot number and a one-line label. |
| **What to record** | The exact frame: which card, list, button, or preview should be on screen. |
| **Where to find it** | Whether it is in the app, the public site, or a physical prop — plus the screen name. |
| **How to navigate** | Step-by-step: the route to type, the sidebar item to click, the button to press, the demo record to open. |

Each script also ends with **Screenshot guidance** (the single "money shot" frame to nail) and **On-screen text** (the captions to burn in). Treat the navigation steps as literal: routes are real, demo names are fixed (see the Demo Account Data Template below), and "where enabled" features should only be filmed if they are actually turned on in the demo account.

## Global edit rules

- Start with the screen already loaded. Do not show dead time.
- Remove page loading delays unless the point is speed.
- Use mouse highlight only for busy screens.
- Add callout boxes only for key workflow moments.
- 2–3 second shots for short form. 5–8 second shots for long-form walkthroughs.
- Prefer real workflow order over feature dumping.
- Show one concrete outcome per scene: appointment booked, consultation started, prescription created, invoice created, follow-up tracked, message ready.
- Add a small "Demo data" label on all patient-facing screens.
- Avoid too many zoom animations. Use them only to direct attention.

## Brand tone

Plain, operational, and founder-led.

Use:

- "Run today's clinic work from one place."
- "Start with one doctor and one front desk user."
- "Replace scattered registers, Excel sheets, and WhatsApp follow-ups with one connected workflow."
- "Designed for Indian private clinics and hospitals."
- "Demo data shown."

Avoid:

- "Revolutionary"
- "AI will run your clinic"
- "Guaranteed"
- "100 percent compliance"
- "No staff training needed"
- "All hospitals must use this"

## Recording setup checklist

Before recording any product script:

- [ ] Demo account loaded with the data template above.
- [ ] Only demo data visible — no real patient names, numbers, or addresses.
- [ ] Browser cleaned: no extensions, bookmarks, or personal tabs.
- [ ] Browser zoom 90–100%.
- [ ] Cursor visible, slow, intentional.
- [ ] Screen recorded at 1920x1080 minimum (16:9 long-form, 1080x1920 shorts).
- [ ] Notifications muted on the recording device.
- [ ] Clean, default browser theme.
- [ ] "Demo data" label ready to overlay on patient-facing screens.

Screen recording software: QuickTime / ScreenFlow / OBS (Mac), OBS / Camtasia / Xbox Game Bar (Windows), Loom (browser), built-in recorder (mobile).

## Editing checklist

Before editing:

- Confirm demo account is clean and no real PHI is visible.
- Record voiceover separately if possible.
- Save raw screen recording, voiceover, and music in separate folders.
- Export one long-form master and one vertical short-form project file.

During editing:

- Remove dead time.
- Add captions, "Demo data" label, chapter labels, contact outro.
- Keep music quiet under voice.
- Use no copyrighted trending songs unless licensed for every target platform.

Export:

- Long form: 1920x1080, 16:9, H.264 MP4, 15–25 Mbps.
- Shorts: 1080x1920, 9:16, H.264 MP4, 10–20 Mbps.
- LinkedIn square variant if needed: 1080x1080.
- Thumbnail: 1280x720 for YouTube, 1080x1920 cover-safe frame for shorts.

Post-recording:

- [ ] Review footage for real patient data (blur if found).
- [ ] Bookmarks bar hidden.
- [ ] No personal notifications appeared.
- [ ] Cursor movement smooth.
- [ ] Demo-data label visible on patient-facing screens.
- [ ] Exported at correct resolution.

## Campaign order

A 7-week launch sequence. Long-form anchors the week; shorts are cut from that week's long-form recording.

**Week 1 — Introduce the product**
- Long: *What Is Docita* (foundational intro, publish everywhere)
- Long: *30-Day Free Trial* (drive signups)
- Shorts: what is docita, 30-day free trial, grab the free trial, why docita is different

**Week 2 — Adoption is easy**
- Long: *Five-Minute Clinic Setup*
- Long: *How to Use Docita Day by Day*
- Shorts: setup speedrun, start from today, one doctor one front desk, clinic setup in one morning

**Week 3 — Doctor value**
- Long: *Doctor Perspective*
- Shorts: doctor interruptions, prescription, follow-up, doctor in 30 seconds

**Week 4 — Owner value**
- Long: *Facility Owner Perspective*
- Shorts: owner visibility, front desk chaos, billing connected, owner dashboard

**Week 5 — The connected journey**
- Long: *Complete Patient Journey*
- Shorts: one patient journey, WhatsApp chaos, paper and Excel to Docita, connected billing

**Week 6 — Front desk value**
- Long: *Front Desk Workflow*
- Shorts: receptionist workflow, appointment to payment, front desk is not the database, front desk in 30 seconds

**Week 7 — Scale and communication**
- Long: *WhatsApp Workflows* or *Hospital Operations*
- Shorts: WhatsApp control, hospital coordination, multi-doctor clinic, indian clinic software

**Weeks 8+ — Expansion themes** (use the new long-form ideas in the long-form file): lab workflow, public booking, import & OCR, analytics deep-dive, ABDM/ABHA where enabled, multi-clinic management, security & trust, objection-handling ("we already use WhatsApp", "we are too small", "my staff won't learn it").
