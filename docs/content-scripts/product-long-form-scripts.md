## How to cut shorts from these long-forms

Each long-form recording is a source for 3–6 shorts. To cut a short:

1. Pull the single scene that proves one idea (e.g., the prescription preview, the queue updating, the invoice auto-total).
2. Open with that scene's idea as on-screen hook text (see [`product-short-form-scripts.md`](product-short-form-scripts.md) for ready hooks).
3. Trim to 15–45 seconds, reframe to 9:16, add captions and the shorts outro card.
4. Reuse the relevant voiceover line; do not re-record unless the pacing needs it.

The short-form file lists, for every short, the exact screens and navigation — so a short can also be recorded standalone if you do not have the long-form footage yet.
