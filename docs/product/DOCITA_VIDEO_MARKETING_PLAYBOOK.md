# Docita Video Marketing Playbook

This document is the working brief for screen-recorded Docita marketing videos. It is written for the founder, voiceover artist, and editor.

Primary CTA:

```text
https://docita.work
support@docita.work
+918790311010
```

## Positioning

Do not open with "please use our software." Open with the operational pain:

- Clinics lose time because appointments, patient notes, billing, prescriptions, and WhatsApp messages live in separate places.
- Doctors do not want more admin work. They want patient context, prescription flow, and fewer interruptions.
- Facility owners want visibility: what happened today, who is waiting, what was billed, and what follow-up is pending.
- Docita should feel like a clinic operating layer, not just appointment software.

Core message:

```text
Docita helps clinics and hospitals run appointments, patient records, consultations, prescriptions, invoices, follow-ups, WhatsApp workflows, and facility operations from one connected workspace.
```

Adoption message:

```text
Start small. Set up one doctor, one front desk user, and today's appointments. You do not need to digitize the entire facility on day one.
```

## Compliance Guardrails

- Use demo data only.
- Do not show real patient names, phone numbers, addresses, prescriptions, lab reports, invoices, ABHA details, or WhatsApp conversations.
- Blur browser bookmarks, email, phone notifications, local file paths, account IDs, API keys, and admin-only diagnostics.
- Avoid claims like "guaranteed revenue increase", "fully compliant for every hospital", or "replaces medical judgment."
- Use the phrase "where enabled" for WhatsApp, payments, patient portal, ABHA, insurance, lab, and hospital workflows if the video is not tied to a specific active plan.
- Include a small footer on clinical demo videos: "Demo data shown. Docita supports clinical workflow documentation and does not replace medical judgment."

## Standard Video Templates

### Long Form Template

Use for YouTube, website embeds, sales follow-ups, and WhatsApp demo links.

Length:

- Short long form: 4 to 6 minutes.
- Full product tour: 8 to 12 minutes.
- Webinar style: 18 to 25 minutes.

Structure:

1. Hook, 0:00 to 0:15
   - State the clinic problem in one sentence.
   - Show a fast visual preview of the final workflow.
2. Context, 0:15 to 0:40
   - Who this is for: solo doctor, clinic, hospital, front desk, owner, or admin.
   - What will be shown.
3. Demo, 0:40 to 80 percent of runtime
   - Move through real screens in workflow order.
   - Use zoom-ins on fields, buttons, queue rows, prescription preview, invoice preview, and WhatsApp templates.
4. Recap, final 30 to 45 seconds
   - Repeat the operational benefit, not the feature list.
5. CTA, final 10 to 15 seconds
   - Show contact card.

Visual format:

- Record screen at 16:9, 1920x1080 minimum.
- Browser zoom 90 to 100 percent.
- Cursor visible, movement slow and intentional.
- Use a clean demo account with no browser extensions visible.
- Editor should add 120 to 140 percent zoom punches for important clicks.
- Use captions for every video.

Audio:

- Voiceover first, music second.
- Music should be low, -24 to -30 LUFS under voice.
- Prefer calm, modern, light, trust-building music.
- Avoid aggressive EDM, comic sound effects, dramatic cinematic risers, or distracting beats.

Outro card:

```text
Run your clinic workflow from one place.

Docita
https://docita.work
support@docita.work
+918790311010
```

### Short Form Template

Use for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, LinkedIn short video, and X video.

Length:

- Best range: 15 to 45 seconds.
- Demo explainer: 45 to 75 seconds.
- Keep ad-style versions under 60 to 90 seconds.

Structure:

1. Hook, 0:00 to 0:03
   - On-screen text must be readable without sound.
2. Proof visual, 0:03 to 0:10
   - Show the screen that solves the problem.
3. Micro-demo, 0:10 to 0:30
   - One workflow only. Do not tour the whole app.
4. CTA, final 3 to 5 seconds
   - Show contact card or pinned caption.

Visual format:

- Edit in 9:16, 1080x1920.
- Keep all text inside the central safe zone.
- Use big captions, 2 lines maximum.
- Use fast but clean cuts: no more than one idea per shot.
- Use zoom crops from the long-form recording.

Caption style:

- Font: clean sans-serif, medium or semibold.
- Color: white or near-white text on a dark translucent strip.
- Highlight one keyword per sentence using Docita brand accent color if available.
- Do not cover buttons, patient names, prescription content, or invoice totals.

Outro card for shorts:

```text
Docita
Clinic workflows in one place
docita.work
```

Footer for caption or post text:

```text
https://docita.work
support@docita.work
```

## Global Edit Rules

- Start with the screen already loaded. Do not show dead time.
- Remove page loading delays unless the point is speed.
- Use mouse highlight only for busy screens.
- Add callout boxes only for key workflow moments.
- Use 2 to 3 second shots for short form.
- Use 5 to 8 second shots for long form walkthroughs.
- Prefer real workflow order over feature dumping.
- Show one concrete outcome per scene: appointment booked, consultation started, prescription created, invoice created, follow-up tracked, message ready.
- Add a small "Demo data" label on all patient-facing screens.
- Avoid too many zoom animations. Use them only to direct attention.

## Brand Tone

Plain, operational, and founder-led.

Use:

- "Run today's clinic work from one place."
- "Start with one doctor and one front desk user."
- "Replace scattered registers, Excel sheets, and WhatsApp follow-ups with one connected workflow."
- "Designed for Indian private clinics and hospitals."
- "Demo data shown."

Avoid:

- "Revolutionary"
- "AI will run your clinic"
- "Guaranteed"
- "100 percent compliance"
- "No staff training needed"
- "All hospitals must use this"

## Editing Checklist

Before editing:

- Confirm demo account is clean.
- Confirm no real PHI is visible.
- Record voiceover separately if possible.
- Save raw screen recording, voiceover, and music in separate folders.
- Export one long-form master and one vertical short-form project file.

During editing:

- Remove dead time.
- Add captions.
- Add "Demo data" label.
- Add chapter labels.
- Add contact outro.
- Keep music quiet under voice.
- Use no copyrighted trending songs unless licensed for every target platform.

Export:

- Long form: 1920x1080, 16:9, H.264 MP4, 15 to 25 Mbps.
- Shorts: 1080x1920, 9:16, H.264 MP4, 10 to 20 Mbps.
- LinkedIn square variant if needed: 1080x1080.
- Thumbnail: 1280x720 for YouTube, 1080x1920 cover-safe frame for shorts.
