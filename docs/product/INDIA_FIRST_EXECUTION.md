# India-First Execution Notes

## Focus
- Lead with multi-specialty private clinics in India, especially 1-5 doctor setups.
- Keep positioning centered on migration, usability, and clinic operations.
- Avoid leading with hospitals, broad AI promises, or multi-country expansion.

## Sales Surface
- Homepage and supporting pages should emphasize:
  - paper-to-cloud migration
  - receptionist-friendly workflows
  - WhatsApp-based patient communication
  - practical trust language over unsupported badges
- Use these pages during outreach:
  - `/paper-to-cloud`
  - `/whatsapp-workflows`
  - `/trust`

## First 90-Second Demo
1. Import or review existing patient data.
2. Show queue and day-of-visit workflow.
3. Generate and share a digital prescription.
4. Show invoice and follow-up communication.

## GTM Notes
- Primary city sequence:
  - Hyderabad
  - Bengaluru, Chennai, Pune
  - Ahmedabad, Coimbatore, Kochi, Visakhapatnam
- Primary channels:
  - founder-led demos
  - WhatsApp shares
  - referrals
  - local partnerships

## Messaging Guardrails
- Prefer "Indian clinics" or "private clinics" over "all facilities" in primary acquisition copy.
- State live features separately from planned features.
- Do not claim formal certifications unless separately verified and documented.
