## Posting Rules

- Do not claim guaranteed revenue, guaranteed compliance, or guaranteed patient outcomes.
- Do not mention real patient data.
- Prefer operational language: appointments, queue, prescriptions, invoices, follow-ups, WhatsApp workflows, front desk, facility visibility.
- Use "where enabled" for WhatsApp, payments, patient portal, lab, ABHA, insurance, and hospital workflows.
- End with one clear action: book a walkthrough, see the demo, start small, or contact Docita.
- Use founder voice often. Clinics trust a clear operator more than a generic brand account.
