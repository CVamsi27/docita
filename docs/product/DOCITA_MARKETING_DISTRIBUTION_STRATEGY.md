# Docita Marketing and Distribution Strategy

This document captures the practical go-to-market strategy for making clinics, hospitals, doctors, front desk teams, and facility owners understand and adopt Docita.

Primary CTA:

```text
https://docita.work
support@docita.work
+918790311010
```

## Core Positioning

Do not sell Docita as "software to use." That sounds like work, migration, training, and disruption.

Sell Docita as a way to reduce clinic chaos:

```text
Run appointments, patient records, consultations, prescriptions, invoices, follow-ups, and WhatsApp workflows from one connected place.
```

The strongest adoption message:

```text
Start with one doctor, one front desk user, and today's appointments. You do not need to digitize the entire clinic on day one.
```

## Distribution Principle

Cold calls alone are weak because clinics hear interruption, not value.

Better distribution is:

- Demo-led.
- Founder-led.
- Workflow-specific.
- Local and referral-driven.
- Built around screen recordings, not abstract claims.

The best CTA:

```text
Show me your current clinic workflow. I will map it inside Docita.
```

## 7. Local Clinic Outreach

Replace cold calls with personalized workflow demos.

Sequence:

1. Find clinic or hospital.
2. Identify likely workflow: solo clinic, multi-doctor clinic, hospital OPD, specialty clinic.
3. Record a 60-second personalized screen demo.
4. Send by WhatsApp/email.
5. Follow up after 2 days.
6. Offer 15-minute walkthrough.

Message:

```text
Not asking you to switch today. I can show your existing appointment-to-prescription workflow inside Docita in 15 minutes.
```

Target list:

- 100 local clinics.
- 25 small hospitals.
- 25 specialty clinics.
- 20 diagnostic/lab-connected clinics.
- 20 multi-doctor OPD facilities.

Track:

- Contacted.
- Replied.
- Demo booked.
- Demo completed.
- Pilot started.
- Rejected reason.
- Follow-up date.

```text

```

Owner pitch:

```text

```

Hospital pitch:

```text
Docita gives hospitals and multi-doctor facilities a connected OPD operating workflow across front desk, doctors, billing, follow-ups, and communication.
```

Objection: "We already use paper and WhatsApp."

```text
That is exactly where many clinics start. Docita does not require a full migration on day one. Start with today's appointments and patients, then move prescriptions, billing, and follow-ups into the workflow gradually.
```

Objection: "We do not have time to change systems."

```text
The first step is small: one doctor, one front desk user, and today's appointments. The goal is to reduce disruption, not create it.
```

Objection: "Call later."

```text
No problem. I can send a 60-second workflow demo first. If it looks relevant, we can schedule a 15-minute walkthrough.
```
